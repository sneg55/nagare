Where the money goes, and what the chain records

Shielding and unshielding are ordinary public deposits. Everything between them happens inside the STRK20 pool, where no address of either party appears.

What vests, and what a cancel takes back

The amount accrues linearly from the day the schedule is funded. The cliff controls only when withdrawal opens, so the whole accrued amount becomes available at once when it passes.

Where the keys come from

One wallet signature produces the seed every Nagare key is derived from, so a new browser rebuilds them all. A key that arrives in a claim link is the exception.
